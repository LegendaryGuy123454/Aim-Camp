# Aim Camp

A quick, browser-based aim trainer. Hit as many targets as you can in 30 seconds, track your accuracy, and try to beat your high score.

## How to play

1. Open `aim-camp.html` in a web browser.
2. Click **Start 30-second round**.
3. Click the target as quickly and accurately as you can. Clicking elsewhere counts as a miss.

The target gets smaller as your score goes up. Your best score is saved in your browser.

## Features

- 30-second arcade-style rounds
- Hit count and accuracy tracking
- Targets that shrink as you score
- High score saved in the browser
- Responsive layout for desktop and mobile
- No installs, accounts, or extra libraries required

## Built with

HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
